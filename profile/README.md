## Prostir

Extra monitors around you in a VR headset. They are real monitors of your PC: windows,
the mouse and the taskbar work with them as with any other screen. The picture comes from
your PC over Wi-Fi or a USB cable.

- **Download:** [latest release](https://github.com/prostir-app/prostir/releases/latest)
- **Website:** [prostir.ishawyha.dev](https://prostir.ishawyha.dev)
- **Questions and ideas:** [Discussions](https://github.com/prostir-app/prostir/discussions)
- **Bugs:** [Issues](https://github.com/prostir-app/prostir/issues)

Open source, written in Rust. Made in Ukraine.
